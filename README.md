# Cetinje Bible / Цетиньская Библия

## Research Project

**Cetinje Bible** is an ongoing research project devoted to the philological, linguistic, and theological analysis of the Hebrew biblical text.

The project develops methods for the structural and semantic analysis of Biblical Hebrew, translation studies, and digital publication of biblical research.

## Repository Contents

This repository contains materials related to the Cetinje Bible project, including:

- analyses of the Hebrew biblical text;
- translation resources;
- TLS/TLS7 materials and experimental publication formats;
- historical editions of *To Christians of the Third Millennium*, originally developed within the Cetinje Bible project.

## Related Projects

The philosophical and theological project originally developed within the Cetinje Bible project has been separated into two independent repositories:

- 📘 **To Christians of the Third Millennium (CTM)**
- 📗 **To Christians of the Third Millennium II: The Architecture of Subjecthood**

Future development of the CTM project continues in those repositories.
